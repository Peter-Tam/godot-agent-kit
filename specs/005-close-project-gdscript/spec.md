# Feature Specification: Safely Close a Clean Project GDScript

**Feature Branch**: `spec/005-close-project-gdscript`

**Created**: 2026-10-01

**Status**: **Complete.** Quality, clarification, design, granularity and consistency gates passed. T001/T002 retain their accepted native/public-caller evidence; T003 passed [cumulative acceptance](quickstart.md#12-t003-cumulative-acceptance-2026-10-02) with [reviewed evidence coverage](quickstart.md#t003-execution-decision-and-evidence-review) for all scenarios, requirements and success criteria. Completion is independent of PR state. Roadmap Phase 1 is **complete** under the subsequent [phase exit decision](../../PHASE_1_EXIT.md).

**Input**: Generated feature description supplied to `/speckit.specify`:

> Safely close one existing standalone project GDScript in an explicitly selected authorized local Godot Editor session once the caller has finished working with its clean, persisted document. Callers can already discover, observe, open and coherently edit scripts, but must still ask the developer to close a tab. Supply that missing Phase 1 lifecycle step and compose with existing opening for close/reopen, without adding Save, discard or history-control commands.

> Before closing an open document, independently establish agreement of disk source, loaded Script source and live buffer, document-attributed clean state, and a current target-bound expected revision/identity basis. Protect those facts at the closing boundary. Refuse unsaved human work, including equal-text-but-dirty state, divergent or stale sources, changed revisions or document/session identities, missing safety observations, ambiguous or unauthorized targets, and unsupported closing effects. Never save, discard, reload, repair, force a prompt, or close a replacement document to make the request succeed. Preserve unrelated documents, unsaved work and native history.

> Use normal editor lifecycle semantics and verify that the intended document is actually closed, its persisted source is unchanged, and any retained loaded Script source remains unchanged. After independently confirmed closure, the buffer is absent, not an observed empty buffer; a released Script is not a reason to force-load it. Acknowledge ordinary native selection and target-document history disposal where they are consequences of closure, without promising an undoable close or history retention after reopening. An already-closed target is a freshly verified no-change result, not evidence that this request closed it or that all editor state is clean.

> Distinguish acceptance, closing initiation, known effects and independent verification. Timeout, cancellation, disconnection and human close/reopen races must preserve known effects and uncertainty, never imply rollback or authorize automatic retries. No interrupted operation may reopen a document as compensation or close a newer buffer. Prove positive closing, dirty/stale/effect refusals, unrelated-work preservation, bounded interruptions, repeated use, and the composed discovery/open/observe/edit/close/reopen workflow with applicable existing A–E, native-history and Save/reparse/rescan/runtime durability guarantees. Preserve local authenticated routing, project confinement, privacy, protocol independence and production-export isolation.

> Exclude independent Save and Undo/Redo controls, save-and-close or discard workflows, batch closing, new file operations, embedded or non-GDScript documents, conflict resolution and force writes, concurrent-agent orchestration, MCP, broader core generalization, runtime/debugger/language-intelligence tools and distribution expansion. Leave interfaces, closing mechanisms, internal design, bounds and exact support choices to planning. No generic lifecycle framework, new service, approval layer, CI topology or operational prerequisite is required without a concrete present need under Principle XIII.

## Clarifications

### Session 2026-10-01

- Q: May closing a script trigger Godot’s normal revalidation of unchanged, already-loaded scripts when source and human work remain protected? → A: Allow guarded native revalidation. Ordinary close-triggered revalidation is permitted with effect checks and independent verification; explicit reload/reparse/rescan actions, source reconciliation, Save, discard and project-code execution remain prohibited.

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

The primary caller is a coding-agent automation layer acting for a developer who may continue using the live editor. The capability closes **one existing standalone project GDScript document**, not an editor window, a project, every tab or the underlying file. It does not close dirty documents, even when their current text equals disk.

**D** is source on disk, **R** is source held by the loaded Godot Script, and **B** is source in the live editor buffer. These remain independent authorities. A **verified newly closed** result requires a safe, current open document before the operation and independently established closure with unchanged persisted source afterward. Once closure is confirmed, B and document-buffer dirty state are not applicable; the result must not claim post-close `D == R == B`. R may remain loaded or be released through ordinary lifecycle behavior, and that distinction must be explicit.

Closing intentionally removes the selected document's buffer. Ordinary native disposal of that buffer's local history and native selection of a remaining tab may follow; this is not permission to discard unsaved source, change retained source, clear unrelated history or invoke Save. The stories are independently verifiable outcomes of one capability, not permission to expose closing before its safeguards exist.

### User Story 1 - Finish Work by Closing the Intended Clean Script (Priority: P1)

As a coding-agent caller, I want to close the clean script I have finished using, so the developer does not have to manage tabs left open by the agent's workflow.

**Why this priority**: Observation can report a closed document and opening can create a tab, but neither performs the missing open-to-closed transition. Closing one safe target is useful without a general document-management feature.

**Independent Test**: In a real Godot Editor, independently establish a clean open target, agreeing D/R/B and its expected revision/identity basis. Request closure, witness the actual absence of its document/buffer and unchanged source, then separately observe and reopen it through the existing capabilities.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an exact authorized project/session and supported open standalone GDScript with independently observed agreeing D/R/B, document-attributed clean state and a matching expected basis, **When** closing is requested, **Then** only that document closes. Verified newly closed is returned only after independent closure and unchanged-source evidence, not merely an editor acknowledgment.
2. **Given** an eligible target open in a non-selected tab and a different selected document, **When** closing is requested, **Then** the exact target closes without first selecting it or changing the other document's selection, source, dirty state or history. When the target itself is selected, ordinary native selection of a remaining document is permitted and reported without further selection actions. Native close-triggered revalidation of unchanged already-loaded source is permitted only with independently verified preservation; an acknowledgment before required continuation evidence is not success.
3. **Given** a valid existing target independently confirmed already closed, **When** closing is requested, **Then** the result reports already closed without request-caused change. It does not load or open the script, alter retained R, claim that this request removed a buffer, or infer project-wide cleanliness. Available D/R evidence and limitations remain explicit.
4. **Given** an otherwise safe clean script whose source is read-only or has a syntax error, **When** closing is requested, **Then** absence of write permission or syntax validity alone does not prevent closure. No source write or repair occurs, and lifecycle success does not claim parse validity or runtime correctness.
5. **Given** a verified newly closed target, **When** the caller separately observes it and then uses existing opening, **Then** observation reports the actual closed state without manufacturing B, and supported reopening preserves the persisted source and restores independently agreeing D/R/B. Closing itself neither reopens the document to verify it nor grants authorization for a later edit.

### User Story 2 - Preserve Human Work and Refuse Stale Closing Intent (Priority: P1)

As a developer, I want an agent to leave my document open when I have unsaved work or its earlier understanding is no longer valid, so closing cannot become a hidden discard or Save.

**Why this priority**: Removing a live buffer can destroy work even without writing disk. Clean-state attribution, current intent and exact document identity are inseparable from unattended closing.

**Independent Test**: Prepare dirty, equal-text-but-dirty, divergent, changed-revision and replaced-document targets with distinctive source and native history. Attempt closure, including changes between initial checks and the closing boundary, and independently verify preservation of buffers, source, dirty state and usable history.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the target has document-attributed unsaved work, including a dirty buffer textually equal to D, **When** closing is requested, **Then** it refuses as a dirty conflict. The document stays open with its source, dirty state and history intact; no Save, discard, merge or confirmation acceptance occurs.
2. **Given** D/R/B disagree, a Resource or buffer is known stale, or required source, dirty or open-state evidence is unavailable, **When** closing is requested, **Then** it refuses with the actual conflict or missing-observation reason. Equality of the remaining authorities does not establish safety, and no reload or synchronization manufactures eligibility.
3. **Given** a source revision, document identity or session changed since the caller obtained its expected basis, or a human closed and reopened the same path with identical text, **When** the older request is attempted, **Then** it does not close the new state or buffer. A missing or unusable expected basis cannot authorize closing an open document. A genuinely already-closed result is permitted only as fresh no-effect recognition, not application of the stale request to a replacement.
4. **Given** human typing, a save, a rename/replacement, a close/reopen or other relevant change occurs after initial checks but before closure, **When** the closing boundary is reached, **Then** detected invalidation prevents closing under stale evidence. Same-text changes do not bypass attributable dirty/version or identity checks. If that boundary cannot be protected, closing is unsupported rather than optimistically allowed.
5. **Given** unrelated documents contain unsaved work and valid native history, **When** an eligible target closes or an unsafe request refuses, **Then** those documents retain their source, dirty state and usable history. A context in which native closing would apply differing pending buffer text must refuse before effects. Already-dirty R == B may remain eligible with independent dirty-state, effect and preservation evidence; closing must not wait for or perform synchronization to manufacture that eligibility. The operation does not apply all pending buffers, save other documents or clear shared history.
6. **Given** selection is ambiguous or replaced, access is denied, the path escapes the project, the file is missing, the document is embedded/non-GDScript, or the closing context would cause unpermitted source/editor/execution effects, **When** closing is requested, **Then** an actionable distinct refusal occurs before those effects. A missing file with a surviving open buffer is not already closed; that buffer remains intact. No candidate source or unauthorized target information is disclosed.

### User Story 3 - Know Whether an Interrupted Close Took Effect (Priority: P1)

As a coding-agent caller, I want unsuccessful closing attempts to report what actually happened and what remains unknown, so I do not blindly retry against a document the developer has since reopened.

**Why this priority**: Losing a response can leave the target closed, still open or reopened under a new identity. A timeout is neither proof of closure nor proof that no effect occurred.

**Independent Test**: Interrupt real requests before closing entry, after closure and during independent verification. Separately witness document identities and source; include human reopening and editing before verification finishes, held responses and an unresponsive editor.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the request is acknowledged or closing has begun but required independent postconditions are missing, **When** a result is returned, **Then** it does not claim verified newly closed. It identifies the reached stage, known buffer/selection/history effects, source evidence and remaining uncertainty.
2. **Given** cancellation, timeout or disconnection occurs, **When** the operation terminates, **Then** the result distinguishes proven no closing effect, known applied or partial effects, and effects unknown. A proven no-effect refusal cannot close later; loss of acknowledgment alone cannot establish that guarantee. Every controlled local attempt, including an unresponsive editor, returns within ten seconds.
3. **Given** closure occurred but a human reopened or edited the target before verification completed, **When** the result is formed, **Then** the newer document and work remain intact. Invalidated closure evidence produces a truthful non-success with known effects, not another close, Save, source restoration or success attributed to the newer buffer.
4. **Given** an interrupted attempt may have closed the document, **When** the caller considers another operation, **Then** it is directed to freshly observe the explicit target first. No automatic retry or compensating reopen occurs; a newly requested close must satisfy current state and intent checks rather than reuse authority over an earlier buffer.
5. **Given** another kit operation overlaps a closing attempt, **When** the attempts reach their effect boundaries, **Then** existing single-editor operation protection is preserved. A refused overlapping request cannot later execute, is not silently queued or retried, and cannot close a document another operation is opening or editing under obsolete evidence.

### User Story 4 - Complete and Repeat the Safe Script Workflow (Priority: P1)

As a developer, I want caller-driven closure to compose with the already-proven script workflow without losing persisted edits, hidden changes to other tabs or weakened history and durability guarantees.

**Why this priority**: A close that works once but loses source on reopening, disturbs unrelated buffers or weakens existing operations does not complete the Phase 1 lifecycle gap.

**Independent Test**: Use real discovery, opening, observation and editing to produce a clean persisted change, close through this capability, and reopen through existing opening. Repeat across safe and unsafe cases; exercise applicable native history, ordinary Save and durability checks with independent witnesses.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a discovered supported script and the completed opening/observation/editing capabilities, **When** the caller explicitly opens, observes, edits, freshly observes, closes and reopens it, **Then** the intended persisted revision remains intact and the reopened D/R/B agree without manual tab preparation, closing or reconciliation. Each operation retains its own target and safety checks.
2. **Given** at least 20 closing requests spanning supported clean open, already closed, dirty and stale/unsafe targets, **When** they run with deliberate intervening human edits and reopenings, **Then** every result reflects its actual document identity and state, with no lost unsaved work or unexpected late closure. At least five requests actually close clean documents, five recognize already-closed targets, and five refuse dirty targets, including equal-text-but-dirty work.
3. **Given** the composed workflow and known native source history, **When** applicable A–E and ordinary Save, close/reopen, reparse, rescan and permitted fixture-runtime checks run, **Then** existing coherent-edit, dirty-refusal, actual Undo/Redo, persistence and sequential-edit guarantees remain satisfied. History transitions are exercised while the relevant buffer exists; target-history retention after ordinary close is not newly promised.
4. **Given** the closing capability is available, **When** existing discovery, observation, opening and editing workflows run, **Then** their public meanings, limits and deadlines remain unchanged. Observation/discovery remain non-interfering, editing still refuses a closed document, and no existing operation implicitly closes a script.
5. **Given** authorized, denied, ambiguous and interrupted close requests with synthetic source sentinels, **When** results, incidental logs, routing metadata and production exports are inspected, **Then** no unauthorized or incidental project content is exposed, unrelated targets are untouched, and no active closing tooling or gameplay dependency is exported.

### Edge Cases

- A human closes and reopens the same path with exactly the same text: a new buffer is not the old close target, and an earlier request must not close it.
- A clean script has useful native history. Closing may dispose of that target buffer's history exactly as ordinary native closure does; refusal and already-closed recognition must dispose of none, and unrelated history remains usable. No synthetic source-history entry or undoable-tab-close claim is introduced.
- A script remains loaded after its tab closes, or ordinary lifecycle release unloads it. Report actual retained or released state; do not force unloading, loading or a source copy to simulate post-close agreement.
- The target file is deleted, renamed, replaced or redirected while a buffer survives. Preserve the buffer and refuse before effects when detected; do not turn disappearance on disk into permission to discard it or close a replacement.
- Empty source is valid observed text. Whitespace/line-ending differences remain differences. Source or representation limits are explicit and never justify truncation, inferred cleanliness or closure without required evidence.
- Closing the last script tab may leave no selected script. Closing the selected tab may natively select another; closing a non-selected tab must not steal selection. No particular fallback tab or general focus-control capability is required.
- A native prompt or unsupported callback context would require Save, discard, unrelated source application or unpermitted execution. Do not answer the prompt or manufacture safety; refuse before effects where detectable, otherwise report actual partial/unknown effects without false success.
- Ordinary native closing may schedule revalidation of unchanged already-loaded scripts. Admit and independently verify that continuation without allowing source reconciliation, new project-code execution or loss of human work; missing or invalidated completion evidence prevents verified success. This is not a separate reparse command or a requirement that the closing target be parse-valid.
- Human or external activity invalidates postconditions after closure. Preserve the newer state and disclose invalidation; do not reclose, reopen or restore source to make the earlier request appear successful.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: The caller MUST be able to request closure of one known, existing standalone project GDScript in one explicitly selected authorized local editor-session lifetime. Closing MUST be an explicit lifecycle operation distinct from discovery, observation, opening and editing, not file deletion, editor shutdown or batch tab management.
- **FR-002**: Target resolution MUST preserve existing authenticated, local, project-confined and lifetime-bound routing. Ambiguous, unavailable, ended/replaced, unauthorized or outside-project targets MUST NOT select by focus, expose candidate source, start an editor or silently substitute a session/document. Effects and evidence MUST remain attributed to the actual intended target.
- **FR-003**: A request that would close an open document MUST carry a usable target-bound expected revision/identity basis from prior observation. Before effects, the system MUST independently establish matching current identity and revision, open state, agreeing D/R/B and document-attributed clean state. Dirty state, divergence, known stale state, missing/invalidated evidence or absent/unusable basis MUST refuse without saving, discarding or repairing. Source equality alone MUST NOT establish cleanliness.
- **FR-004**: Expected state and safety checks MUST remain valid at the closing boundary. Detected source, dirty/version, namespace, document or session changes MUST NOT authorize stale closure, including a same-text replacement/reopened buffer. Existing single-editor kit-operation protection MUST prevent competing effects and late execution of refused attempts. Inability to protect the boundary MUST produce unsupported/refused state rather than an assumed guarantee.
- **FR-005**: Closing MUST use Godot-native lifecycle semantics as the canonical route and close only the admitted target. It MUST NOT write source, Save, discard unsaved work, synchronize differing authorities, explicitly reload/reparse/rescan, apply differing pending buffer text, execute project code or accept a destructive prompt. Ordinary close-triggered revalidation of unchanged already-loaded scripts MAY occur only under effect checks and independent verification that source, dirty state and unrelated history remain preserved; this exception MUST NOT authorize repair, new project-code execution or a separate validation capability. Required native continuations remain part of the attempt's verification, not unreported work after an acknowledgment. A context whose required effect safety cannot be established MUST refuse before dispatch. Ordinary target-buffer removal, target-local native history disposal, possible Script release and native selection following closure remain permitted lifecycle effects, not permission for broader side effects.
- **FR-006**: Verified newly closed MUST require fresh independent evidence that the exact intended document closed and no target buffer remains open, D retains the admitted persisted source, and any retained R remains independently observable with unchanged source. Ordinary release of R MUST be distinguished from inability to observe retained state; no force-load is required or permitted. Confirmed closure makes B and document-buffer dirty state not applicable, never observed empty/clean. Missing, divergent or detectably invalidated required postconditions MUST prevent success; an acknowledgment or missing buffer read alone is insufficient.
- **FR-007**: A valid existing target confirmed already closed MUST produce a distinct no-change result from fresh evidence without load/open/close, source, selection, dirty or history effects. An open-document basis is not required solely for no-effect recognition, but any supplied basis MUST NOT be transferred to replacement state or described as verified application. Available D and already-loaded R and their limitations MUST remain explicit; recognition does not assert coherence, clean retained R, absence of other unsaved work or successful completion of an earlier interrupted close. Unknown open state and missing/invalid files MUST NOT be classified as already closed.
- **FR-008**: Unrelated documents MUST retain source, dirty state, open state and usable native history. Closing a non-selected target MUST preserve the current selection; closing the selected target MAY allow ordinary native selection of a remaining document or no script selection, and the effect MUST be reported. Refusals and already-closed results MUST change no history. Successful close MUST add no source-history action or claim an undoable close; any target-local history disposal MUST be confined to ordinary native closure and documented, not generalized to shared/unrelated history.
- **FR-009**: Each attempt MUST distinguish request acceptance, closing initiation, known lifecycle application and independent verification. Results MUST expose requested/resolved target or refusal, expected and observed revisions/identities where applicable, reached stage, known closure/selection/history effects, D/R/B availability and synchronization, dirty state, relevant available diagnostics, evidence interval/validity and partial failures. Unavailable facts MUST have reasons; post-close inapplicability MUST NOT conceal a failed observation or unknown open state.
- **FR-010**: Structured outcomes MUST distinguish verified newly closed, already closed without request-caused change, refused with no closing effect, applied/partly applied but unverified, and effects unknown. Reasons MUST distinguish dirty conflict, revision/identity mismatch, divergence, known stale Resource/buffer, missing required evidence, ambiguous target, missing/invalid/unsupported document, unsafe closing context, denied access, unavailable/disconnected editor, timeout and cancellation wherever applicable. Syntax errors or read-only source alone MUST NOT make an otherwise safe clean target uncloseable; available parse diagnostics are separate from lifecycle success.
- **FR-011**: Every controlled local closing request MUST return a terminal result within ten seconds, including an unresponsive-editor case. Timeout, cancellation and disconnection MUST NOT imply rollback or definitely-not-applied state. A no-effect outcome requires evidence that the request did not cause effects and cannot cause them later; otherwise uncertainty MUST be explicit. Existing operation deadlines remain unchanged.
- **FR-012**: The system MUST preserve newer human work during closing and verification. It MUST NOT close a replacement/reopened buffer, restore older source, Save, reopen as compensation or otherwise force postconditions after invalidation. Known effects and safely attributable evidence MUST remain explicit in non-success outcomes without concealing unexpected changes.
- **FR-013**: Closing attempts MUST NOT automatically retry, queue a refused action for later execution or replay against replacement state. After possible application, the caller MUST be directed to fresh observation before another action. A new request against a still-closed target may follow FR-007, but this does not establish unconditional replay safety across reopening or session replacement.
- **FR-014**: Existing source limits and availability/attribution distinctions MUST remain unchanged. Unsupported closing profiles or missing safety observations MUST refuse before effects where detectable; unexpected loss of required post-close evidence MUST produce an unverified result with known effects. Source MUST NOT be truncated, copied between authorities or normalized into false agreement, and independently observed empty source MUST remain distinct from unavailability.
- **FR-015**: Completed discovery, observation, opening and editing MUST preserve their existing meanings, bounds and safety behavior. Supported callers MUST be able to compose those operations with closing and existing reopening without manual closing or source reconciliation. Closing MUST NOT introduce implicit lifecycle behavior into another operation, a second mutation policy, MCP exposure or gameplay authority; the existing protocol-independent ownership boundaries remain in force.
- **FR-016**: The capability MUST preserve local-only operation, authentication, project confinement, source-free private routing metadata, no telemetry and existing permission boundaries. Only explicitly requested authorized results may contain selected source evidence; incidental logs/metadata MUST NOT disclose project contents, unauthorized paths or credentials. No arbitrary-filesystem, remote/network, execution, force or approval-bypass capability is introduced. Development/export isolation MUST remain explicit and verified for closing.
- **FR-017**: Real-editor acceptance MUST demonstrate positive clean closing, already-closed no-change recognition, dirty/stale/unsafe refusals, protected identity transitions, unrelated-work/history preservation, truthful bounded interruptions and repeated composed use. Applicable existing A–E/native-history and Save/close-reopen/reparse/rescan/runtime durability guarantees MUST remain satisfied. Pure decision logic and changed boundaries/transitions MUST receive deterministic isolated coverage. Refusing every new close, or relying only on disk/headless/mocked evidence, does not satisfy the capability.
- **FR-018**: Exact supported Godot versions/environments and verified limitations MUST be recorded from applicable CI and real-editor evidence before support or feature completion is claimed. Previous feature evidence is a baseline, not proof that closing works. Any inapplicable surface or scenario MUST have a substantive recorded justification; unavailable required observability is not a passing result.

### Outcome Interpretation

These meanings describe caller-visible behavior, not command syntax, a wire schema or a new transaction framework.

- **Verified newly closed**: The request caused the admitted open-to-closed transition and all required independent postconditions passed. This describes the observed interval, not a promise that a human cannot reopen afterward.
- **Already closed, unchanged**: Current evidence establishes that the valid target has no open document and this request caused no change. Retained loaded-source or other observation limitations remain explicit; this is not retroactive proof about a prior request.
- **Refused, not applied**: Evidence establishes no request-caused closing/lifecycle effect and that this attempt cannot apply later. The reason and permitted relevant evidence remain available.
- **Applied or partly applied, unverified**: Some lifecycle effect is established but required closure, source-preservation or independent verification conditions did not pass. Known changes are not erased by calling the operation unsuccessful.
- **Effects unknown**: Evidence cannot establish whether or how far the request applied. Missing acknowledgment, inaccessible editor state or timeout alone does not establish absence of effects.

Closed is a document-lifecycle fact, not proof that the Script is unloaded or that the project is clean. A retained R is observed from its own authority; a genuinely unloaded R has the existing explicit unavailable/unloaded meaning. B becomes not applicable only when absence of the document is independently established. Neither an outcome nor an earlier observation authorizes source mutation or action against a newer buffer.

Mutation-semantic and security-sensitive planning/review must explicitly record constitutional compliance.

**Principle XIII boundary:** The present gap is that an agent can create and use a script tab but cannot close it through the kit; asking a human to do so leaves the roadmap lifecycle capability absent. An unguarded close can discard dirty work or remove a newer buffer, and read-only observation cannot itself perform closure. The simplest credible scope is one explicit guarded close using existing routing, observation and common safety semantics. Its necessary cost is close-boundary protection, lifecycle outcome verification and real-editor transition evidence, justified by safely removing that manual step. No generic document manager, background service, alternate history, new dependency, approval mechanism, CI provider/runner topology or operational prerequisite is selected. Planning must justify any additional mechanism against the concrete current need, simpler alternatives, existing limits, ongoing cost and why it is necessary now.

Closing creates no source edit or reversible-close claim, so it must not invent a native source-history entry. Ordinary target-buffer history disposal is permitted only as the normal close consequence; it does not weaken existing edit Undo/Redo guarantees while the buffer exists or preservation of unrelated histories. Applicable A–E remain required for the composed workflow, including actual native source Undo/Redo, dirty refusal, caller-driven close followed by existing reopening, and sequential editing. Ordinary Save/history interactions and permitted runtime launches remain acceptance actions, not new product commands. Verification scope follows the repository's affected-boundary and cumulative-gate rules; headless execution, disk checks and acknowledgments cannot prove live-buffer closure or preserved human work.

### Key Entities *(include if feature involves data)*

- **Closing target**: One authorized local project, live editor-session lifetime and standalone script/document identity; a path alone cannot distinguish a reopened buffer from the prior one.
- **Closing intent and expected basis**: The caller's request to remove the observed clean document, bound to its current source and identity rather than permission to save, discard or act on a future replacement.
- **Lifecycle evidence**: Attributable before/after open state, document identity, independent D/R/B, dirty/version facts, retained/released loaded state, revisions, selection/history effects and observation validity or limitations.
- **Closing outcome**: Reached stage, new closure versus already-satisfied state, known/partial/unknown effects, independently verified postconditions or distinct refusal/interruption reason, and safe next action.

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: In every supported clean-open acceptance case, including selected/non-selected targets, read-only source and a safe syntax-error fixture, exactly the intended document closes without manual intervention. Every verified result matches independent closure and unchanged-source witnesses; no source write, Save, discarded unsaved work or guessed target occurs.
- **SC-002**: Every dirty, equal-text-but-dirty, divergent/stale, changed-basis, missing-evidence, replaced-target, denied/escaping or unsafe-effect case refuses without operation-caused source, buffer, dirty, selection or history changes. Boundary-race cases preserve the newer human state, and already-closed cases cause zero lifecycle or source effects.
- **SC-003**: Every interruption and post-close human-reopening case reports effects and uncertainty consistent with independent witnesses, with zero false verified-close, definite-no-effect or rollback claims. Every controlled local attempt, including an unresponsive editor, terminates within ten seconds, and no proven refusal applies later.
- **SC-004**: At least 20 real-editor closing requests include five successful clean closures, five already-closed recognitions and five dirty refusals, with deliberate intervening edits and reopenings. No unsaved work is lost, no old request closes a new buffer, unrelated history stays usable and no source is changed by closing.
- **SC-005**: A caller can discover an eligible script, open/observe/edit it, obtain fresh state, close it and reopen it through existing opening without manual path lookup, tab management or reconciliation. The persisted intended revision survives reopening and applicable Save/reparse/rescan/runtime checks; existing A–E and native-history acceptance remain satisfied.
- **SC-006**: Existing discovery, observation, opening and editing acceptance retains its specified outcomes, limits and deadlines. Refusals and no-change closes add or remove zero history entries; successful closes affect history only as documented ordinary disposal of the target buffer, never unrelated documents.
- **SC-007**: For every acceptance case, a reviewer using only the result can identify the target or refusal, whether closure was new/already satisfied/partial/unknown, the applicable or unavailable D/R/B and dirty evidence, selection/history effects and the safe next action. ## Assumptions

- Features 001–004 are complete according to current task and acceptance records. Their existing behavior is reused, not reopened for implementation. Normal prerequisite delivery sequencing still governs later dependent work; this specification starts no implementation task.
- The caller can name one exact standalone script and authorized local project/session. A close with effects uses a prior target/revision basis plus fresh boundary checks. No-effect recognition of a currently closed target needs no invented open-buffer basis and grants no authority over a later reopened buffer.
- Conflict policy is refusal, not save-before-close or discard. A human may resolve unsaved state separately, after which the caller must obtain fresh evidence. Permission or approval never replaces safety observation.
- Normal close may dispose of the target buffer's native history and may leave its Script loaded. This feature does not promise persistent history across closing/reopening, unload control or reversal of a tab close; preservation of unsaved source and unrelated work remains mandatory.
- Clean-state closure is not a parse-validation feature. Positive ordinary standalone-script behavior, including the safe invalid-source and read-only cases, must be demonstrated; declared support limits cannot reduce the feature to refusing every target. The permitted guarded revalidation of unchanged already-loaded source is native close bookkeeping, not a parse-validity requirement for the closing target or permission for explicit repair/reparse operations.
- Ten seconds bounds one controlled local closing result, following existing lifecycle/mutation timing, not arbitrary project size, concurrent throughput or completion of later separate operations. Planning defines reproducible supported fixtures and bounds.
- Human edits and external changes remain possible. Detected invalidation must prevent false success, but this feature introduces no cross-editor leases, distributed coordination or new claim of atomic exclusion against arbitrary non-cooperating external writers.
- APIs, expected-basis representation, closing mechanisms, internal boundaries, transport evolution, supported limits, exact compatibility and evidence collection remain planning decisions under the existing architecture and complexity gate. No new stack, engine requirement or support matrix is selected here.
- This draft and its quality checklist establish requirements only. Approval, clarification if needed, planning, task derivation, granularity review, analysis, implementation and real-editor acceptance remain separate work; no feature-completion or Phase 1 exit claim follows specification creation.
